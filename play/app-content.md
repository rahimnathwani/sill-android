## Ads
No, the app does not contain ads.

## App access
**All or some functionality is restricted** → instructions for the reviewer:

> The app is a client for Sill, a free Mac app (https://getsill.app). Without a Mac running Sill on
> the same network the app shows its connect screen only. To review it: install Sill on a Mac with
> Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later, open it, put the phone on the same Wi-Fi, open this app and
> tap the Mac; the Mac shows a 12-digit code to type on the phone. No account or login exists.

(Play may still reject an app it cannot exercise. A short screen recording of pairing and streaming,
linked here, helps.)

## Content rating
Questionnaire category: **Utility, productivity, communication or other**. Answer No to every
content question (no violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling); user
interaction: No (the app only connects to the user's own Mac; no chat, no sharing with others);
shares location: No; digital purchases: No. Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

## Target audience
18 and over (it is a tool for a Mac's owner; choosing children's ages brings Families policy
obligations for no reason).

## Data safety
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
  Play's definition of "collect" is data transmitted off the device to the developer or a third
  party. This app sends data only to the user's own Mac, which the user controls and pairs
  explicitly; nothing reaches the developer or anyone else.
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? **Yes** (TLS 1.3 to the Mac).
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted? **Not applicable** (nothing
  is collected); uninstalling deletes everything the app stored on the phone.

## Government apps, financial features, health, news
No to each.

## Permissions declarations
None needed: no sensitive permissions (no SMS, call log, location, accessibility, all-files access,
foreground service, or QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES).
