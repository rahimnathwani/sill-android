## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Use your Mac from your Android phone. Sill for Android is an unofficial client for Sill, the free
Mac app that streams your Mac's windows to a phone. Install Sill on your Mac from getsill.app, open
this app on the same Wi-Fi, tap your Mac and type the code it shows. That's it.

• Stream your whole Desktop, or just one window: pick it from a list with live thumbnails.
• Touch controls that feel natural: tap to click, drag to scroll with momentum, hold and drag to
  select or move, two-finger tap to right-click, pinch to zoom in on small text.
• Draw mode turns your finger into a pressed mouse button, for whiteboards and drawing apps.
• A real keyboard: your phone's keyboard, plus a key row with Esc, Tab, ⌘ ⌃ ⌥ ⇧, arrows and a
  Spotlight key. Hardware keyboards work too.
• Away from home: with Remote Access on in Sill and a VPN such as Tailscale, connect by address.
• Private by design: the phone talks only to your Mac, encrypted with TLS 1.3, both ends checking
  each other's key. No account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later and the free Sill app (getsill.app).

Not made by Sill's developer. Sill for Android is an independent, open-source client
(Apache-2.0): github.com/rahimnathwani/sill-android
